# Museek 1.3.1

- Play now stays in the horizontal center of the app. Previous sits on its left, with Next and Stop on its right; trimming and resizing keep Play centered.
- Previous and Next keep the current artwork and song details visible while the next track loads, then reveal the new content together with a short fade and slide. The controls stay in place when artist or album tags are missing.
- Track animations respect the Windows animation setting and release their animation clocks after use. Playback and seek updates retain their existing allocation limits.
- The native audio decoder now initializes in the background when audio is first opened, allowing the player window to appear sooner, stay responsive during first-time loading, and use less memory when idle.
- The selected volume is reapplied when audio output starts, including the first track.

Install this version over your existing Museek installation. Your settings and audio files are kept.
